Q Questions

Questions about plans and permits.

Not on the list? Write to the studio and we answer by email.

Email the studio
How is the final price set?

Package prices are starting prices. The final quotation depends on the project scope, your requirements and the actual site conditions, and we send it before any work starts.

Are the plans signed and sealed?

Yes. The plans in the building permit package are signed and sealed by qualified professionals, ready to file.

What is an as-built plan for?

It documents an existing structure as it stands today. You need one to process a building permit or an occupancy permit for work that is already built.

Do you design condo units and small shops?

Yes. The condo and interior package covers units and rooms, and we also design commercial fit-outs and kiosks.

Can you help with the DOLE CHSP application?

Yes. It is an add-on to the building permit package, together with structural and electrical analysis.

W Process

Four steps from first message to signed plans.

  1. Step 01

    Project brief and location

    Message us the location, the size and what you want built or documented. Photos of the lot or unit help.

  2. Step 02

    Site visit and measurements

    We see the lot, unit or building in person. For as-built work we measure it as it stands today.

  3. Step 03

    Layouts, 3D views and plans

    Layouts and 3D views come first for your approval, then the full set of plans for each trade.

  4. Step 04

    Signed and sealed plans

    Qualified professionals sign and seal the plans, and we prepare the permit forms for filing.
